--- 01_Module_and_Syllabus ---
* MoSHE_General_Physics_Module_Phys1011_2019_OFFICIAL.pdf
  THE official national Freshman General Physics module (Ministry of Science
  and Higher Education, Oct 2019, 201 pages). Used by ALL Ethiopian
  universities. Covers: Preliminaries, Kinematics & Dynamics, Fluid Mechanics,
  Heat & Thermodynamics, Oscillations & Waves, Electromagnetism & Electronics,
  Cross-cutting Applications. Authors: W. Anjulo (Arbaminch), B. Mengistu
  (Haramaya), W. Leul (Hawassa), T. Bekele.
* Phys1011_Course_Syllabus_WCU.pdf - official Phys 1011 course syllabus
  (4 pages): objectives, weekly breakdown, grading (Mid 40% / Final 40% /
  Assignment & Lab 20%).
