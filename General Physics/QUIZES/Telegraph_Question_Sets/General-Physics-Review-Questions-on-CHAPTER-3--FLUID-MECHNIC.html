5. Lead has greater density than iron and both are denser than water. Is the buoyant force on a lead object greater than or lesser than or equal to the buoyant force on an iron object of the same volume? Explain your answer giving the reason.

Solution

- Buoyant force can be defined as the upward force acting upon the immersed object.

- As Lead has greater density than iron but both have the same volume.

- Both will observe the same amount of buoyant force on them.

Explanation:-

The buoyant force is independent of the nature of the immersed object:- If two objects of the same density are immersed in the same type of liquid (say water ) the object will observe the same amount of buoyant force as it is independent of the nature of the immersed object.


6. When an object is immersed in a liquid at rest, why is the net force on the object in the horizontal direction equal to zero?

Solution

The pressure is the same on points that are at the same level but on opposite sides. So net force along horizontal is equal to zero.
