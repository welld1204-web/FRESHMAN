PART I: MULTIPLE CHOICES (24%)
Instruction: Choose the best answer and write your choice in Capital letter on the space provided for each question.

1. A car starts from rest and uniformly accelerates to a final speed of 20.0 m/s in a time of 15.0 seconds. How far does the car travel during the time?
   A. 150.0 m   B. 600.0 m   C. 400.0 m   D. 300.0 m

2. Consider three vectors, A = 3i − 3j, B = −i − 4j and C = −5i − 5j; if rA = kB + C = 0, the scalars r and k respectively are ________.
   A. 2 and 3   B. 2 and 4   C. 1 and 2   D. 2 and 2

3. A 20 g bullet is fired into a stationary block of wood having mass 8 kg. The bullet imbeds into the block. The speed of the bullet-plus-wood combination immediately after the collision is 1 m/s. What is the original speed of the bullet?
   A. 402 m/s   B. 401 m/s   C. 400 m/s   D. 399 m/s

4. As shown in the figure (in original), a ball on a table of height h is thrown horizontally and the ball is rolled off the table traveling at a speed V, neglecting air resistance. How far (D) from the bottom of the table does the ball fall?
   A. v[h/g]^(1/2)   B. hv/2g   C. v[2h/g]^(1/2)   D. 2h

5. A block of mass m is pulled along a horizontal floor with a rope that is maintained at an angle θ above the horizontal. The coefficient of friction between the block and the floor is μ. What force is required to maintain the motion of the block at constant speed?
   A. μmg   B. μmg·tanθ   C. μmg/cosθ   D. μmg/(cosθ + μ sinθ)

6. Starting from point A an object moves along the grid through points A, B, C, E and stops at F as shown below (in original). The side of the square tiles measures 0.1 km. The distance covered by the moving object and the magnitude of the displacement of the object respectively are ________.
   A. 0.7 km and ([1.3]^(1/2))/10 km
   B. 1.3 km & 0.5 km
   C. 0.5 km & 1.3 km
   D. ([1.3]^(1/2))/10 km & 0.7 km
